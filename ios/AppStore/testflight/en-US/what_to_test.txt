Please focus on the complete sync and progress workflow:

1. Sign in to MyPIU with Google or Discord.
2. Connect a PIUGAME Phoenix or Phoenix 2 account and run a foreground sync.
3. Confirm the Home, Songs, Progress, and Activity views match the linked player's data.
4. Search for songs, open chart history, favorite a song, and review recommendations.
5. Relaunch without a network connection and confirm cached catalog data remains usable.
6. Reconnect, sync again, then test both PIUGAME disconnect and MyPIU account deletion.
7. Enable the included Safari extension, grant access only to MyPIU and the selected PIUGAME site, and trigger a browser sync.

Please report the device model, iOS version, selected PIUGAME version, and the affected song or chart with any issue.
